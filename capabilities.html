LanTech Technologies Capability Overview

Mission-Focused Engineering and Secure Infrastructure Support

LanTech Technologies delivers secure, scalable, and mission-aligned IT solutions for federal, defense, and commercial environments. We provide infrastructure engineering, network modernization, cybersecurity support, and data-focused technical services designed for performance, reliability, and operational continuity.

Technical focus: Dell PowerEdge and Hyper-V; Active Directory and SharePoint; Windows and RHEL; PowerStore and Unity storage; Cisco ASA and VLANs; Apache NiFi; Splunk and Grafana. These areas reflect the founders’ technical backgrounds; engagement scope and delivery resources are agreed for each project.
